Character

04

Principles

How I decide.

P1

Motion only to explain

Animation is diegetic or it's deleted. A scroll should feel like the visitor's own breath moving through the product — never a fireworks show.

P2

Honest states, no mock demos

An unwired checkout says "in preparation". A roadmap page shows the roadmap. Placeholder testimonials are labeled placeholder. Trust compounds.

P3

Constraints are design inputs

薬機法 advertising law, trade-secret pricing, factory workflows, PDP privacy law — I design with the constraint from day one instead of patching around it later.

P4

A lot with a little

A 7-variant configurator in three dependency-free HTML files. A 755-line scroll engine instead of a library. Complexity must be earned by the problem.
